What we do in the office

What happens in our session

Each session is 3 hours. It teaches one idea and ends with something the student made.

  1. MindsetOwnership, discipline and no blame.
  2. One ideaExplained in plain words.
  3. PracticeA simulation or game to try it safely.
  4. Real taskWork with the office team on a real job.
  5. PresentExplain the work and get feedback.

What we are learning

Six areas, each taught by doing.

How a business works

Customers, problems, products, money in and out, and who does what.

Example: map how a property is rented from first call to signed contract.

Real estate operations

Onboarding tenants, contracts, cheques, follow-ups and complaints.

Example: write a tenant onboarding checklist the team can use.

Money skills

Pricing, commission, VAT basics, invoices and simple budgets.

Example: prepare an invoice and check the totals.

Communication and sales

Calls, emails, WhatsApp replies, negotiating and presenting.

Example: handle a practice call with a customer.

AI as a work tool

Drafting, summarising, templates and helper bots. Students must understand and defend what AI gives them.

Example: build a reply template, then improve it by hand.

Professional habits

Being on time, finishing tasks, taking feedback and owning mistakes.

Example: run a short weekly review of their own work.

Questions parents ask

Who is it for?

Students who learn well by doing, and families who want practical business and AI skills. Tell us your child’s age and grade and we will say if it fits.

Does it replace school?

That is your family’s decision. Some students come full time, others add it to their other learning. We can talk through the options with you.

How do you track progress?

Every session ends with a finished piece of work and a short review. Parents see the work and the feedback.

Are the children’s work and photos kept private?

We show a child’s name, photo or work on this site only with the family’s written approval. We never show client names, prices or documents.

How do we start?

Send us a message. We will arrange a visit to the office so you and your child can see a session.
